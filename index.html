From shortlist to decision

How to use a Sugargoo spreadsheet

Keep the exact product option, measurements and unanswered questions together so a saved link becomes useful research.

01 / FIND & VERIFY

Open the original details

Choose a category and compare similar items. Open the CAFANS detail page, locate the available source information and confirm the exact color, model and quantity.

  • Save the listing URL and checked date.
  • Confirm current stock and the selected variant.
  • Ask your agent whether the specific item can be purchased and shipped to your destination.
02 / MEASURE & REVIEW

Compare measurements, then photos

Compare clothing measurements with a garment you own. For shoes, distinguish foot length from insole length and confirm the seller’s sizing method.

  • Record chest, length, waist or inseam as relevant.
  • When QC images are available, check the selected size, color, quantity and visible condition.
  • Request clarification on missing details before proceeding.
03 / BUDGET & CONFIRM

Plan the full cost

A product price is only one part of the amount you may pay. Obtain current quotes and record costs separately before committing to an order.

  • Include domestic delivery and agent charges.
  • Compare parcel shipping quotes for the actual destination and package.
  • Review exchange costs and any applicable destination charges.

Make your sheet useful

What to save alongside each product

A Sugargoo spreadsheet is most helpful when it preserves the exact option you researched. Export this catalog selection and add columns for seller, original source URL, color, size, measurements, quantity, checked date and agent eligibility. Keep the source currency beside any conversion so you can refresh the estimate later.

Use separate notes for seller statements and your own observations. A product photo can show a color or visible detail, but it does not confirm fabric composition, durability or authenticity. Record missing information as a question rather than filling it with an assumption.

Give each item a research status: considering, awaiting measurements, awaiting an agent quote or ready for review. Before paying, reopen the listing and confirm the selected option, current price and applicable return terms. An older spreadsheet row remains a reference point rather than a current offer.

Good questions, direct answers

Sugargoo spreadsheet FAQ

What is a Sugargoo spreadsheet?

A Sugargoo spreadsheet is a product research list used to organize finds, source links, prices, sizes and shopping notes. A listing in a spreadsheet does not guarantee that Sugargoo can purchase or ship the item.

Where do the products on this page come from?

The 12 displayed entries were taken from the CAFANS homepage catalog on November 19, 2026. Names, product images and estimated USD prices are source snapshots, not live inventory or checkout quotes.

Do these buttons place an order through Sugargoo?

No. “Sugargoo spreadsheet” and “Buy it now” both open CAFANS. Product links open the corresponding CAFANS detail page. Confirm the original listing and agent eligibility before paying.

Does the displayed price include shipping?

The displayed amounts are CAFANS estimated USD item prices. Shipping, agent fees, exchange costs and destination charges may be additional.

Can I download the product list?

Yes. Select your filters and use “Export CSV” to save the visible products, categories, reference prices, CAFANS detail URLs and snapshot date. Add your own measurements and purchasing notes in your preferred spreadsheet app.

Are these QC photos or verified product reviews?

No. The pictures are source catalog images. No independent testing, authentication or warehouse QC inspection is claimed. If your agent provides inspection photos, check that they show your exact order and ask about anything unclear.

What if an item is unavailable or cannot be purchased?

Recheck the current source listing and ask the agent about that specific item. Keep unavailable or ineligible entries marked in your own sheet and choose another product if necessary.
